The right conversation at the right time changes what people spend their lives on.

Princeton Silicon Valley TigerTrek™ is an annual trip that brings 22 Princeton students to Silicon Valley for off-the-record conversations with industry legends. Year after year, this trip has transformed the ideas and career goals of both trip participants and students back on campus.
