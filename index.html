The right conversation at the right time changes what people spend their lives on.

Princeton Silicon Valley TigerTrek™ is an annual trip that brings 20 Princeton students to Silicon Valley for off-the-record Q&A's with industry legends. Year after year, this trip has transformed the ideas and career goals of both trip participants and students back on campus.

Why SVTT?

Testimonies

However, the University environment does not offer too many chances to break the "Orange Bubble," especially when it comes to entrepreneurship. TigerTrek filled in that gap, allowing me to connect and learn from both entrepreneurial legends and Princeton peers I would have not met otherwise. This made this program one of the most valuable aspects of my Princeton education.

I am so grateful for the diverse perspectives and the sense of community I found in my fellow trekkers!"
